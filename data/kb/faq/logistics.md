## What if I miss the call?
No problem. If you miss an interview call, you can call the interview line back and the assistant
will identify you and either continue where you left off or help you reschedule.

## Can I reschedule?
Yes. You can reschedule any stage up to 12 hours before the start time with no penalty. Just tell
the interview assistant or reply to the scheduling email.

## What timezone are interviews in?
All interview times are scheduled in India Standard Time (IST) unless your recruiter has agreed
otherwise in writing.

## Do I need to prepare anything specific?
Bring your own experience and be ready to reason out loud. For coding and design rounds, a quiet
space and a stable connection matter more than any specific preparation.

## Accessibility
If you need any accommodation for an interview, tell your recruiter or the assistant and we will
arrange it.

## Who do I contact for help?
The interview assistant can connect you with the recruiting team at any point during a call.
