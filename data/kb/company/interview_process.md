## Overview
The Backend Engineer loop has four stages after the initial recruiter screen: a phone technical
screen, a coding round, a system design round, and a values interview. The full loop is usually
completed within two weeks.

## Stages
1. Recruiter screen (30 min): role fit, logistics, compensation expectations.
2. Technical phone screen (45 min): fundamentals and one focused coding problem.
3. Coding round (60 min): two problems, correctness and clarity over cleverness.
4. System design round (60 min): design a realistic system, discuss trade-offs.
5. Values interview (45 min): candor, ownership, craft, and momentum through past examples.

## Rescheduling
You can reschedule any stage up to 12 hours before the start time at no penalty by replying to
the scheduling email or telling the interview assistant. Missed interviews without notice are
rescheduled once as a courtesy.

## Results Timeline
Decisions are communicated within three business days of the final round. Feedback is shared at a
high level regardless of outcome.
