## Working Hours and Flexibility
We operate on a flexible schedule built around a core-hours window of 11:00 AM to 4:00 PM IST,
during which teammates are expected to be reachable. Outside core hours you manage your own
time. We measure output, not hours logged.

## Remote and Hybrid Work
The company is remote-first. Employees may work fully remote from anywhere in India, or use one
of our offices in Bengaluru and Pune. Fully-remote hires receive a one-time home-office stipend
of INR 40,000 and a monthly internet reimbursement of INR 1,500.

## Paid Time Off
Full-time employees receive 24 days of annual paid leave, 12 public holidays, and 10 days of
sick leave. Parental leave is 26 weeks for primary caregivers and 6 weeks for secondary
caregivers. Unused annual leave carries over up to a maximum of 10 days per year.

## Company Values
We hold four values: candor (say the hard thing kindly), ownership (you build it, you run it),
craft (quality is a feature), and momentum (ship, learn, repeat). These values show up directly
in how we interview and evaluate.

## Learning and Development
Every employee has an annual L&D budget of INR 60,000 for courses, books, and conferences, plus
two paid conference days per year.
