## How Compensation Works
Compensation is set by level, not by negotiation theatrics. Each level has a published band;
offers land within the band based on interview signal and current-level calibration. We review
compensation once a year and on promotion.

## Backend Engineer Bands (annual, INR)
- L3 (Engineer): 18–26 lakh base, plus 5–8% target bonus.
- L4 (Senior Engineer): 28–40 lakh base, plus 8–12% target bonus.
- L5 (Staff Engineer): 42–58 lakh base, plus 12–15% target bonus.
- L6 (Principal Engineer): 60–80 lakh base, plus 15–20% target bonus.

## Equity
All engineers receive stock options vesting over four years with a one-year cliff. Option grants
scale with level; the exact grant is included in the written offer.

## What Is Not Negotiable
Signing bonuses are offered case by case for notice-period buyouts only, capped at two months of
base. We do not counter competing offers with ad-hoc raises outside the band.
