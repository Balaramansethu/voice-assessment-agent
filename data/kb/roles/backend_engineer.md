## Mission
Backend Engineers build and operate the services that power our product: APIs, data pipelines,
and the systems that keep them reliable at scale.

## Responsibilities
- Design, build, and own backend services end to end, including on-call for what you ship.
- Model data and design schemas that stay correct as requirements evolve.
- Write clear, tested code and thorough design docs for non-trivial changes.
- Debug production issues and drive fixes, not just patches.

## Required Skills
- Strong fundamentals in data structures, algorithms, and concurrency.
- Practical experience with a relational database and query performance.
- Comfort designing systems with clear trade-offs around consistency, latency, and cost.
- Familiarity with at least one of Python or Go in a production setting.

## Leveling
- L3 executes well-scoped tasks with guidance.
- L4 owns features and small systems independently.
- L5 owns critical systems and influences team-wide technical direction.
- L6 sets direction across multiple teams and mentors senior engineers.
